Ground-Up Construction Loans

Land & Vertical Construction Financing for New Residential Builds

From lot acquisition to final inspection, Blink Capital Partners finances the construction of new 1-4 unit residential properties with a draw schedule built around your build timeline.

Get Pre-Qualified See Loan Details

Financing Built for Builders and Developers

Ground-up construction loans finance both the acquisition of land (or payoff of an existing lot) and the full vertical construction budget for a new 1-4 unit residential property. Rather than funding the entire construction budget upfront, funds are disbursed through a structured draw process tied to inspected, completed work — keeping the project properly capitalized at every stage.

This program is built for experienced builders and investors developing new construction spec homes, infill lots, and small multifamily (2-4 unit) new builds intended for sale or long-term hold.

Who This Program Is For

  • Builders and developers constructing new 1-4 unit residential properties
  • Investors with an owned or under-contract buildable lot
  • Projects with defined plans, specs, and a construction budget
  • Investors building to sell or building to hold as a rental (DSCR takeout available)

Ground-Up Construction FAQs

What is a ground-up construction loan?

A ground-up construction loan finances the acquisition of land and the vertical construction of a new residential property, with funds released in draws as construction milestones are completed.

Can I finance the lot purchase and construction together?

Yes. Ground-up construction financing can typically cover both land acquisition and the full construction budget in a single loan structure.

How do construction draws work?

Construction funds are released in stages tied to completed and inspected work, rather than disbursed as a single lump sum, protecting both the investor and the capital provider as the project progresses.
