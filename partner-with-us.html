Partner Program FAQs

Do I need to be a licensed mortgage broker to refer deals?

No. Our referral program is open to mortgage brokers, real estate agents, contractors, attorneys, financial advisors, and other professionals who work with real estate investors. Licensing requirements, if any, depend on your role and state.

Is there a cost to become a referral partner?

No. There is no cost or obligation to join our partner program.

How and when do I get paid?

Referral compensation is paid once a referred loan closes and funds. Your dedicated partner contact will walk you through the specific compensation structure when you join.

Will you go around me and work directly with my client?

No. Once you refer a deal, we keep you informed throughout the process so you stay the trusted point of contact for your client's real estate needs.
