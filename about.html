Our Story

Blink Capital Partners was founded with a simple premise: real estate investors deserve a lender who moves as fast as the market does.

We've seen too many deals fall apart — not because the numbers didn't work, but because the lender was slow, unresponsive, or buried in paperwork requirements that had nothing to do with the quality of the asset. We built Blink to be the opposite of that.

We're a private real estate lending company that works with investors doing fix & flips, ground-up construction, DSCR rentals and bridge financing. We underwrite based on the deal — the property, the numbers, and the borrower's plan — not a stack of income or tax return documentation.

If you want a lender you can actually reach, who gives you a straight answer fast and stays with you until the deal closes — that's Blink.

Our Mission

To give residential real estate investors a fast, reliable, and transparent path to capital — so a strong deal never stalls because financing couldn't keep up.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Exclusively focused on non-owner-occupied investment properties
  • Dedicated advisor for every loan, start to finish
  • Relationships matter more than transactions
  • Programs built for fix and flip, construction, and rental hold strategies
  • Process designed to keep pace with competitive offers

Our Values

Speed with discipline. We move quickly without cutting corners on underwriting.

Transparency. Clear terms and no surprises at the closing table.

Partnership. We measure success by how many deals — and how much growth — we help our investors achieve.
