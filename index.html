Common Questions

Investor Financing, Explained

What is a DSCR loan?

A DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan qualifies a rental property based on the income it generates rather than the borrower's personal income or employment history. It's a common financing tool for buy-and-hold residential real estate investors.

What property types does Blink Capital Partners finance?

We finance non-owner-occupied 1-4 unit residential investment properties, including single-family homes, duplexes, triplexes, and fourplexes.

How fast can I close on a fix and flip loan?

Closing timelines depend on the deal, but our streamlined underwriting process is built to move quickly so you can compete for time-sensitive acquisitions.

Do you lend directly or through partners?

Blink Capital Partners originates and services your loan relationship from start to finish.
