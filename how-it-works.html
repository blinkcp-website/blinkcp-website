How It Works

A Streamlined Path From Application to Funded Deal

We built our process around how investors actually operate — fast decisions, clear communication, and funding that keeps pace with your timeline.

1. Apply

Submit your deal details — property address, purchase price, and project scope — through our online application. No obligation, no upfront cost.

2. Get Your Term Sheet

A dedicated advisor reviews your deal and issues a term sheet outlining loan amount, structure, and pricing, typically within 2 hours.

3. Underwriting & Closing

We coordinate appraisal, title, and final underwriting in parallel to keep your closing timeline on track.

4. Fund & Draw

Purchase funds disburse at closing. Rehab or construction funds release through draws as milestones are completed and verified.

What You'll Need

Documents to Have Ready

Having these on hand when you apply helps your advisor move your term sheet faster.

  • Purchase contract or property information (address, price)
  • Scope of work / renovation or construction budget (if applicable)
  • Entity documents (LLC operating agreement, EIN letter) if borrowing through an entity
  • Government-issued ID
  • Proof of funds for down payment / reserves
  • Recent bank statement(s)
Draw Requests

How Draws Are Handled

For fix and flip and ground-up construction loans, rehab and construction funds are released in stages rather than all at once.

  • Submit a draw request once a milestone or phase of work is complete
  • An inspection verifies completed work
  • Approved draws are released promptly to keep your project moving
  • Your dedicated advisor tracks draw status with you throughout the project
