General

What kind of properties does Blink Capital Partners finance?

We finance non-owner-occupied 1-4 unit residential investment properties, including single-family homes, duplexes, triplexes, and fourplexes, through our fix and flip, ground-up construction, and DSCR rental loan programs.

Is Blink Capital Partners a direct lender?

Blink Capital Partners is a loan originator that manages your financing relationship from application through funding and servicing.

What states does Blink Capital Partners lend in?

We fund loans nationwide across nearly every state. Contact us to confirm availability in your specific state.

Do you lend to first-time investors?

Yes. While experience can affect leverage and pricing on some programs, we work with both first-time and experienced investors.

Can I close in an LLC or other entity?

Yes. Our loan programs are business-purpose loans and are typically closed in the name of an LLC or other business entity.

Loan Programs

What is the difference between a fix and flip loan and a DSCR loan?

A fix and flip loan is short-term financing for purchasing and renovating a property to sell or refinance. A DSCR loan is long-term financing for a rental property, qualified using the property's rental income rather than a short-term exit.

Can I refinance a fix and flip or construction loan into a DSCR loan?

Yes. Many investors use a DSCR loan as the long-term takeout financing once a fix and flip renovation or new construction project is complete and the property is rented or ready to rent.

Is there a prepayment penalty?

Prepayment terms vary by loan program and capital partner. Your advisor will review the specific terms of your loan before you commit.
