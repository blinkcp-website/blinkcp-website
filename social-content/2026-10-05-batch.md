## Post 3 — Friday — Market / Thought-Leadership Hot Take

**Format:** Single static image (navy/gold branded graphic, no photo)

**Hook:** "The '10-loan limit' isn't a law of real estate. It's one lender's overlay."

**Caption:**
Hot take: the "10-loan limit" that conventional lenders love to bring up isn't a law of real estate investing. It's just a rule their own underwriting imposes.

Conventional financing caps how many properties you can have financed at once. DSCR loans don't ask that question — they qualify each property on its own rent versus its own debt payment, loan amounts from $100K to $2M, up to 80% LTV, 30-year fixed.

If you've been told you're "maxed out" and it's really just one lender's overlay talking, that's a financing problem, not a portfolio problem.
