## Post 3 — Friday — Market / Thought-Leadership Hot Take

**Format:** Single image, bold statement graphic

**Hook:** "Land loan. Construction loan. Two closings, two lenders, two ways to lose your lot."

**Caption:**
Hot take: splitting your land purchase and your vertical construction budget across two different lenders is how good infill deals fall apart.

Sellers on a buildable lot aren't waiting around for you to line up separate financing for the dirt and the build. And once you're under contract, a construction lender who wants to see the land loan already closed elsewhere just adds a second underwriting process, a second closing, and a second point where your timeline can slip.

Our ground-up construction program is built to be one lender, one process: land funds released at closing, construction funds following a milestone-based draw schedule (framing, mechanicals, drywall, finishes, final inspection) — up to 90% of total project cost, not to exceed 70% of ARV, on 12-24 month terms.

If you're a builder or developer juggling separate land and construction financing right now, that's friction you don't need to be carrying into a competitive bid.
