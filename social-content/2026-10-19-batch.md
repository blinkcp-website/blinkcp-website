## Post 1 — Monday — Educational Carousel

**Format:** Carousel, 5 slides

**Hook (Slide 1):** "Your rehab budget doesn't fund at closing. Here's how it actually releases."

**Slide breakdown:**
1. Hook slide — navy/gold: "Your rehab budget doesn't fund at closing. Here's how it actually releases."
2. "Fix & flip: a 3-stage draw schedule — demo/rough-in, finishes, final punch list."
3. "Ground-up construction: more sequential work, more stages — framing, mechanicals, drywall, finishes, final inspection."
4. "Each draw releases after a passed inspection — on our programs, typically within 48 hours."
5. CTA slide: "Know your draw schedule before you close, not after. blinkcp.com"

**Caption:**
First-time flippers and builders are often surprised by this: your rehab or construction budget isn't handed over at closing. It releases in draws, tied to verified progress — not a calendar, not a lump sum.

On a fix & flip, that's usually 3 stages: demo/rough-in, finishes, final punch list. A ground-up build has more sequential work to verify, so it typically runs 5 stages — framing, mechanicals, drywall, finishes, final inspection. Either way, the sequence is the same: your crew finishes a stage, an inspection confirms it, funds release. On our programs, that release typically happens within 48 hours of a passed inspection — which is the difference between a crew that keeps moving and one that sits idle waiting on money.

Every day a draw is delayed is a day of carrying costs with no work getting done. Know the schedule and what triggers a release before you break ground or start demo.

## Post 4 — Sunday — Myth-Busting / Engagement

**Format:** Carousel, 4 static slides (navy myth → 2 supporting slides → navy CTA)

**Hook (Slide 1):** "Myth: you can cash-out refinance your rental the day the rehab's done."

**Slide breakdown:**
1. Navy myth slide: "Myth: you can cash-out refinance your rental the day the rehab's done."
2. "Truth: most DSCR cash-out refinances require seasoning — commonly 6 months — and it's measured from your purchase closing, not from when the rehab finished or a tenant moved in."
3. "A rate-and-term refi is different — often no seasoning requirement at all, since you're not pulling equity."
4. Navy CTA: "Plan your BRRRR timeline around the real clock. blinkcp.com | 631.353.7022"

**Caption:**
A mistake we see constantly in BRRRR plans: investors assume the refinance clock starts when the rehab wraps or a tenant signs a lease. It doesn't.

Seasoning — the minimum ownership period most DSCR lenders require before a cash-out refinance — is measured from your original purchase closing, specifically the date your deed was recorded. Six months is the most common requirement you'll run into shopping DSCR cash-out refis, though some lenders offer shorter or longer windows tied to different leverage caps. An investor who takes four months to finish a rehab and place a tenant has already burned through most of that clock before the property is even generating income.

Worth knowing: a rate-and-term refinance (no cash out, just better terms) typically doesn't carry the same seasoning requirement, since you're not extracting equity.

Planning your next BRRRR recycle? Know your lender's seasoning rule before you close on the acquisition, not after the rehab's done. blinkcp.com or DM us your timeline.
