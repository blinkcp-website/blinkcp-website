**Slide breakdown:**

2. Photo slide — tag "THE CEILING": "Projected ARV: $340,000 — the number that sets how much leverage is available in the first place."
3. Photo slide — tag "THE LOAN": "Total loan amount: $208,650 — 90% of purchase + 100% of rehab, still inside the 75%-of-ARV cap. 12-month term."
4. Navy CTA: "Know your spread before you bid. Sample deal scenario, not a guarantee of terms. blinkcp.com | 631.353.7022"

**Caption:**
Every flip comes down to the same equation: what you're putting in versus what it's worth when you're done. Here's what that looked like on a real Columbus, OH deal.

All-in cost: $185,000 purchase + $62,000 rehab = $247,000.
Projected ARV once the cosmetic rehab (kitchen, both baths, flooring, exterior paint) was done: $340,000.
Financed: $208,650 — 90% of purchase plus 100% of the rehab budget, comfortably inside the 75%-of-ARV ceiling that caps every fix & flip file we underwrite.

That gap between $247K all-in and $340K ARV is the whole game — it's what pays the loan off, covers your carrying costs, and leaves a margin. The ARV number isn't just a formality for underwriting; it's the ceiling your entire deal is built under.

This is a sample deal scenario, not a guarantee of terms for every borrower. Running your own numbers on a deal? blinkcp.com or DM us the address.
