## Post 1: Educational Carousel — "DSCR Loans in 4 Slides"

**Format:** Carousel (4 slides)

**Hook line (Slide 1):** "Your tax returns say one thing. Your rental property says another. Here's the loan that listens to the property."

**Caption:**
DSCR loans qualify the property, not you.

Instead of digging through 2 years of tax returns and pay stubs, a DSCR loan asks one question: does the rent cover the debt payment?

Swipe through the math ➡️

Slide 2: DSCR = Monthly Rent ÷ Monthly Debt Payment (PITIA)
Slide 3: Example — $2,650 rent ÷ $2,020 payment = 1.31 DSCR. Rent covers the payment with room to spare.
Slide 4: No W-2s. No employment verification. No cap on how many properties you can finance. Just the numbers.
